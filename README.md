# Xiaoyi Wu — Personal Website
Hello, welcome!

Here is my website showcasing research in **optimization and machine
learning** and **applied spatial data science**, with applications in
urban mobility systems.

🌐 **Website:** https://xiaoyiwu21.github.io/

## About

My research connects real-world data, mathematical modeling, and
optimization to support better decisions under uncertainty.

My doctoral work at the Technical University of Denmark covers
demand-responsive transport design, autonomous fleet management, and
dynamic tolling. My applied data science experience builds on my
Master of Urban Spatial Analytics at the University of Pennsylvania
and research experience at Wharton.
